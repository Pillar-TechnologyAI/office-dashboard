Office Dashboard

A private, self-hosted calendar display for one household.

Office Dashboard is a small app that runs on a home network storage device and shows the owner's Google Calendar events on a TV screen: a clock, today's schedule and the rest of the work week.

It requests read-only access to Google Calendar. It cannot create, change or delete events, and it does not access any other Google data.

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner on their own home network.
